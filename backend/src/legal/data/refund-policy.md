This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains what happens to your money when you subscribe to,
change, or cancel the **hosted offering** of Invoicerr, and states plainly, before you pay, the two
facts a subscription carries: it **renews automatically**, and a cancelled period's unused portion is
**never refunded or prorated**. It supplements the [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) — in
particular Section 12 (Term, Cancellation and Termination by You) and Section 13 (Suspension for
Non-Payment, End of Subscription, Data Archive and Deletion) — and does not restate everything those
sections already say. Where this Policy and the Terms of Service could be read differently, the Terms
of Service govern.

## 1. Scope

This Policy covers only the **hosted offering** — the paid subscription operated at our own domain(s).
It does not apply to the **self-hosted** software: Invoicerr's source code is, and remains, free and
open source under the license published in the `LICENSE` file at the root of the
[repository](https://github.com/invoicerr-app/invoicerr). Running it on your own infrastructure
involves no payment to us, so no refund question arises.

## 2. Who Charges You

Subscription payments are processed by **Polar Software Inc.** ("Polar"), which acts as the
**merchant of record** for your subscription (Terms of Service, Section 7.1). In practice, this means:

- the charge appears on your bank or card statement under Polar's name, not ours;
- Polar issues the invoice for your subscription Fee, and calculates, collects, and remits any VAT or
  similar tax due at checkout; and
- your use of Polar's checkout and billing portal is also subject to Polar's own terms.

We are the provider of the Service — see the [Legal Notice](./legal-notice.md) for our identity — but
Polar is who actually takes the payment.

## 3. Auto-Renewal

**Your subscription renews automatically.** Whether you chose the monthly or the annual billing
cycle, it renews at the end of each period, for the same cycle, at the per-Seat rate then in effect,
until you cancel it (Terms of Service, Sections 6.1, 6.4, and 12.1). We do not send an individual
warning before each renewal charge — cancelling ahead of a renewal date is how you stop it; see the
next section.

## 4. How to Cancel, and When It Takes Effect

You can cancel at any time, from the Polar customer portal (Settings > Subscription > Manage
subscription) or by writing to **contact@invoicerr.app**. Cancellation takes effect at the **end of
the period you have already paid for** — the end of the current month or year — and you keep full
access to paid features until then (Terms of Service, Section 12.2). After that, your Company enters
the read-only suspension described in Terms of Service, Section 13.1.

## 5. No Refund, No Proration

**Cancelling does not entitle you to a refund, in whole or in part, for the period you have already
paid for, and we do not prorate a partial period.** If you cancel partway through a month or a year,
the subscription keeps working until that period's own end date; nothing is credited back for the
time you no longer use it (Terms of Service, Section 12.2). This applies equally to a monthly and an
annual subscription, and however far into the period you cancel.

## 6. Seat Changes Mid-Cycle

The hosted offering is billed **per Seat** (Terms of Service, Section 6). Your Seat count is kept in
sync with your Company's actual membership throughout the billing period, and a change to that count
is reflected in the Fees charged for your **next** billing cycle, unless Polar's own billing
mechanics apply the change sooner (Terms of Service, Section 7.3). In practice: adding a Seat
mid-cycle can trigger an immediate, prorated charge from Polar for that Seat, billed the moment it is
added; removing a Seat mid-cycle does not generate a credit or refund for the remainder of the
current billing period — the lower Seat count only reduces what you pay starting with your next bill.

## 7. The Free Trial

New Companies receive a **fourteen (14)-day free trial**, with no payment card required — the trial
itself gives rise to **no payment of any kind**, so no refund question can arise from it (Terms of
Service, Section 3.2). During the trial, every feature of the Service is available except the ability
to send or issue documents. If no paid subscription is activated before the trial ends, your Company
enters the same read-only suspension described in Section 4 above (Terms of Service, Section 13.1).

## 8. No Right of Withdrawal

The Service is offered strictly business-to-business. By subscribing, you represent that you are
acting for purposes within your trade, business, craft, or profession, and not as a consumer
(Terms of Service, Section 1.2). Accordingly, the consumer-protection provisions of the French *Code
de la consommation* do not apply to your subscription, and in particular you have no statutory right
of withdrawal (*droit de rétractation*) in respect of it.

## 9. What This Policy Does Not Limit

Nothing in this Policy limits a right you hold under a mandatory rule of law that this Policy cannot
lawfully exclude, or our own liability for a failure to perform our obligations under the Terms of
Service.

## 10. Failed Payments and Chargebacks

If a renewal or a Seat charge fails, your Company can enter the **Payment failed** status and, if the
failure is not resolved, the read-only suspension described in Terms of Service, Section 13.1 —
update your payment method from the Polar customer portal to fix it. If you believe you were charged
in error, **contact us first** at **contact@invoicerr.app** before raising a chargeback with your bank
or card issuer: most billing questions can be resolved directly, and faster, that way.

## 11. Questions

Questions about this Policy, a charge, or a cancellation can be sent to **contact@invoicerr.app**.

## 12. Governing Language

This document is drafted and executed in English. Where we provide a translation into another
language for your convenience and understanding, that translation is not a substitute for the English
text: in the event of any inconsistency, ambiguity, or conflict between the English version and a
translated version, **the English version prevails** and is the version that governs the rights and
obligations of the parties. Translations are provided in good faith to help each audience understand
